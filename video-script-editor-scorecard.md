### Rewrites for failing lines:

**Line 1 (original):** "CrossOver drops a custom crosshair on top of any game you play."
**Problem:** Generic product description. No emotion, no surprise. Sounds like a readme.
**REWRITE:** "One app. One crosshair. Pinned to your screen no matter what you're playing."
| Rewrite | 8 | 8 | 8.0 | ✅ |

**Rationale:** "Pinned to your screen" is more physical/visual. The repetition of "One" creates rhythm. "No matter what" implies universality without listing.

**Line 4 (original):** "Resize it. Recolor it. Colorblind mode built in. Works on Windows, Mac, and Linux."
**Problem:** Feature list delivery is flat. Needs more personality.
**REWRITE:** "Make it bigger. Make it pink. Colorblind? Sorted. And yeah — Windows, Mac, Linux. All of them."
| Rewrite | 8 | 8 | 8.0 | ✅ |

**Rationale:** "Make it pink" is unexpectedly specific and fun. "Sorted" is casual/confident. "All of them" at the end is a flex mic-drop on cross-platform support.
