# CrossOver Promotional Video Script

**Issue:** LAC-279
**Format:** 30-45 second short-form video (vertical + landscape cuts)
**Platforms:** X/Twitter, YouTube Shorts, Reddit, Microsoft Store
**Writer:** Marketing Lead (script-writer phase)
**Revision:** 4 (editor-approved final)

---

## HOOK OPTIONS (pick one per platform — test all four)

### Hook A — "The Robbery" (Controversy)
> **[SCREEN: Someone clicking "Buy" on a $5 crosshair overlay]**
> **VO:** "You're paying five bucks for a crosshair. A crosshair."

### Hook B — "The Roast" (Pain Point)
> **[SCREEN: Montage of terrible default crosshairs from popular games — tiny white dots, fat blocky crosses]**
> **VO:** "Your crosshair looks like it was drawn in MS Paint."

### Hook C — "The Flex" (Value Shock)
> **[SCREEN: Rapid-fire scroll through dozens of CrossOver's crosshair categories — reticles flying past]**
> **VO:** "Five hundred and thirty crosshairs and it costs you absolutely nothing."

### Hook D — "The Dare" (Challenge)
> **[SCREEN: Black screen, text appears one word at a time]**
> **VO:** "Name one crosshair overlay that's free, has 500 sights, and runs on every OS. I'll wait."

---

## BODY (follows any hook — 20 seconds)

> **[SCREEN: CrossOver launching, clean UI, crosshair appearing over gameplay]**
> **VO:** "One app. One crosshair. Pinned to your screen no matter what you're playing."

> **[SCREEN: Quick cuts — switching between wildly different crosshairs: tactical reticle → neon dot → chevron → custom meme image dragged in]**
> **VO:** "Swap between real optics, dots, chevrons — or drag in literally any image. Your logo, a pizza emoji, whatever."

> **[SCREEN: Split screen — same crosshair consistent across Valorant, Apex, Fortnite, CS2]**
> **VO:** "Same crosshair across every game. Valorant. Apex. CS2. Even the ones with garbage defaults."

> **[SCREEN: Quick settings flash — color picker, size slider, opacity control, multi-monitor toggle]**
> **VO:** "Make it bigger. Make it pink. Colorblind? Sorted. And yeah — Windows, Mac, Linux. All of them."

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Music:** Uptempo electronic/lo-fi beat, drops on Hook, swells on Proof Beat
- **Pacing:** Fast cuts (1-2 seconds per shot max), gameplay footage should feel snappy not cinematic
- **Text overlays:** Bold, clean sans-serif. White with dark shadow for readability over gameplay
- **Tone:** Confident indie dev, not corporate. Think "a friend texting you about a tool they found"
- **Total runtime:** ~35 seconds (fits YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels, and X)
- **Aspect ratios needed:** 9:16 (vertical primary), 16:9 (YouTube/Store embed), 1:1 (X fallback)
